What is it?

Catch falling toppings in a tilting bowl. When two of the same topping touch, they merge into something pricier. The more you stack, the more the bowl tips — and anything that hits the floor costs you its full price!

  • One-finger controls, 1–3 minute runs
  • Score = Sales − Losses
  • Unlock the Seafood Bowl, Moonlit Soba, World Ramen and Cosmic Bowl
  • Plays offline, 9 languages, iPhone & iPad
